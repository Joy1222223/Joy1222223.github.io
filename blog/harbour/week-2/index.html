SCAD X HARBOR - Week 2

Hello, this is my second week of the Scad x Harbor blog!

Based on the suggestions provided by our mentors, we have made some changes.

The most important change is achieving a consistent tone, and we have adjusted all of the shots to a cool tone, which makes them more cohesive. We don't need the gold particles shot because it is not closely related to our overall storyline.

Storyboard:
We have a new storyboard. For shot 2B, we changed it to show a person using tongs to pour the molten platinum into a pot.

Molten Platinum Previs:

This represents platinum, but it does not depict the real appearance of molten platinum. To maintain a consistent tone throughout all the shots, I referenced the creation of molten metal effects from a watch advertisement. We will combine this with live-action footage. Next, I will differentiate the liquid exposed to air from the rest, ensuring that the exposed part forms a thin film on the surface.
