Molten Platinum:

After discussion, we decided to change Shot 01 and Shot 02 to full CG. For Shot 02, we decided to show molten platinum being poured into the mold, the shape of which is part of the watch. The next shot will feature water being poured onto the watch, followed by the bubble shot. This change makes the storyline flow more smoothly, and the viewer can better understand the process of creating a watch. Also, we deleted Shot 05, which is about composing a watch.

Test 1 (Render)

The edges of the fluid are still a bit chunky, and at the beginning, the liquid intermittently breaks apart. I am still working on improving it.

Test 2 (Flipbook))

This is the Flipbook of the pouring simulation. The edges look better, and I also added smoke. I adjusted the angle of the pouring cup and reduced the point separation to achieve more detailed pouring.

I changed the operation to Gaussian, which makes the fluid smoother.

Smoke:

I searched the internet, and according to reality, molten platinum reacts with air and generates steam. Based on this, I selected the surface of the fluid as the smoke source.
