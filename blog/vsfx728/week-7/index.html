VSFX 728 - Week 7

Hello, this is my final project of the VSFX 728's blog!

May 4

Reference:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Gonz0Zr6U8

For my final project, I plan to use the MPM to simulate tomato sauce pouring from a pot into a bowl. Before starting the project, I’ve identified several key considerations. The first is accurately representing the viscosity of the tomato sauce, as it plays a crucial role in how the fluid moves and interacts with the environment. Additionally, I need to account for the presence of tomato chunks within the sauce, which introduces complexity in the simulation due to their interaction with the fluid and the bowl.
